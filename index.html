Alien Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Alien Keyboard is a translating keyboard published by AlienWave Tech Solutions. Because a keyboard can see everything you type, this policy sets out exactly what the app does with your text, what leaves your phone, and what does not.

The two versions work differently, so this policy is in two parts. The iPhone and iPad version translates entirely on your device and sends nothing anywhere. The Android version sends the sentence you dictate to Google's translation service. Read the part that matches the phone you are using.

iPhone and iPad

The short version. Nothing you type leaves your device. Translation runs on the device itself, using Apple's built-in translation. There is no server involved — not ours, not Google's, not anyone's. We have no account system, no ads and no analytics, and we never receive or store anything you type.

Translation. When you press Translate, the text you typed is translated by iOS on the device. Each language pair is downloaded once from Apple and then works with no internet connection at all. The text is never transmitted.

Speaking. The keyboard itself has no microphone access — iOS does not permit custom keyboards to record audio. If you use the microphone inside the Alien Keyboard app to speak instead of typing, what you say is turned into text by Apple's own speech recognition, which may process it on Apple's servers, exactly as dictation on the system keyboard does. That is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. We never see it, and no audio is captured, stored or transmitted by us.

Clipboard history. Up to ten recent copies are kept so the clipboard panel can offer them for pasting. They are encrypted on your device with a key held in the iPhone's keychain, they are never transmitted, and they are not included in backups. You can clear them at any time in the app, under Keyboard settings.

Learned words. Words you type that are not in the built-in dictionary are remembered on your device so suggestions improve. They never leave it.

Your settings. Chosen languages, theme, key size, sound and vibration preferences are stored on your device only.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Android

Everything below applies to the Android version only.

The short version. We do not run a server. We have no account system, no ads and no analytics. We never receive or store anything you type. Two things leave your phone, and only when you ask for them: the sentence you dictate, and a clipboard entry you tap to translate. Both go to Google's translation service so it can translate them, and nowhere else.

What leaves your phone

Text you dictate. When you press the microphone button and speak, the resulting text is sent to Google's translation service to be translated into your chosen language. The translated text is then typed into whatever app you are using.

A clipboard entry you choose to translate. The clipboard panel lets you press and hold a saved entry to translate it. Only entries you press and hold are sent. Simply opening the panel, or tapping an entry to paste it, sends nothing.

Both are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Nothing else you type is ever transmitted. We do not receive a copy — the text goes from your phone to the translation service directly, and AlienWave has no server in the path and keeps no record of it. Once translated text reaches Google, Google's own privacy policy governs what happens to it.

Your voice

The app requests microphone permission, but the app itself never records or receives audio. Pressing the microphone hands the request to your phone's own speech recognition service — on most phones, the Google app — which listens and returns text. Your voice goes to that service under its own privacy policy, not to us. No audio is captured, stored or transmitted by Alien Keyboard.

What stays on your phone

Some things are stored on your device so the keyboard can work. None of it is ever sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Recent clipboard entries. Up to ten recent copies, so the clipboard panel can offer them for pasting. Kept only on your device.
  • Learned words. Up to 400 words you have typed that were not in the built-in dictionary, so suggestions improve over time. Kept only on your device.
  • Your settings. Chosen languages, theme, key size, sound and vibration preferences.

You can erase this at any time:

  • Clear clipboard — in the keyboard's clipboard panel — deletes the saved copies immediately.
  • Forget learned words — in the app's settings screen — deletes the learned dictionary immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • RECORD_AUDIO — required by Android before the phone's speech service may listen on the app's behalf. The app does not read the microphone itself.
  • INTERNET — to send text for translation.
  • VIBRATE — for the key-press tick, if you enable it.

The app also asks Android which speech services are installed, so it can pick one that supports your language. That list never leaves your phone.

Sending feedback

The settings screen has a Send feedback button. It opens your own email app with your phone model, Android version and current language settings already filled in, so a bug report has the details we need. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself, and you can edit or delete any of it first.

Children

Alien Keyboard is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects your data, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
